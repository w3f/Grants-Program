## Project Overview

### Overview
Voultra is a Substrate-based DAO charity and referral protocol. 1% of all platform fees are directed to UNHCR/UNICEF via on-chain community voting. It includes an anti-Sybil referral system with staking to prevent fake accounts.

Building from Bizerte Sud, Tunisia to serve the global Polkadot ecosystem.

- GitHub: https://github.com/voultra-labs/voultra-substrate
- X/Twitter: https://x.com/VoultraLabs

### Project Details
**Problem:** Charity in Web3 is not transparent, referral systems have Sybil attacks.

**Solution:** 
- Pallet charity-dao: Treasury collects 1% fees, token holders vote on-chain to allocate to verified charity addresses (UNHCR, UNICEF).
- Pallet referral-staking: Users stake DOT to generate referral codes, slashing for Sybil.

**Tech Stack:** Substrate, FRAME, Rust, Polkadot.js
**Ecosystem Fit:** Social impact use-case for Polkadot, open source for all parachains.
